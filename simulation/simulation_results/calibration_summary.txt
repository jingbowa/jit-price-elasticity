========================================================================
CALIBRATION SUMMARY: Literature-Based DGP Configurations
========================================================================

Each DGP is parameterized within a random-coefficients logit framework:
  u_ijt = gamma_i + alpha_i * p_jt + kxi_jt + epsilon_ijt
  gamma_i = gamma_0 + gamma_1 * ln(income) + gamma_2 * nu_1
  alpha_i = alpha_0 + alpha_1 * ln(income) + alpha_2 * nu_2
  kxi_jt  = kxi_j + 0.5 * delta_kxi_j   (product fixed effects)
  p_jt    = p_base + p_z_scale * z_jt + p_kxi_scale * kxi_jt

gamma_0 is calibrated so mean |own-price elasticity| matches the
target from the literature. All other parameters are set directly
from published estimates or chosen to produce realistic market
shares and price variation.
